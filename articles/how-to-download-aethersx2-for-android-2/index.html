How to Download AetherSX2 for Android

Published October 19, 2026

What is AetherSX2?

AetherSX2 is a PlayStation 2 emulator for Android that lets you play PS2 games on your phone. It is known for strong performance on mid-range and flagship devices.

Before you start

  • A phone with a decent processor works best
  • You need your own game files (BIOS and ISO)
  • Make sure you have enough free storage

Getting started

Once your device is ready, you can download AetherSX2 and begin configuring it for your games.

Final notes

Emulation works best when you own the original games. Take your time with the settings for smooth gameplay.
